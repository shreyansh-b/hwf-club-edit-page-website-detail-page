Our Story

Honoring traditions, uniting our neighborhood.

The Friends of Fairview Fire Fighters Association flag program connects residents with a shared sense of patriotic pride. Every dollar raised supports critical safety equipment, emergency training, and neighborhood relief funds.

  • Commercial-grade 10-foot aluminum poles with 3'×5' embroidered flags

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about subscribing, installation, and holiday deliveries.

How and where is the ground sleeve installed?
Our volunteers mark and install a durable 1.5-inch PVC sleeve flush with the soil line, approximately 2–3 feet back from the curb or sidewalk. It includes an unnoticeable cap so your lawnmower blade passes directly over it without risk.
What happens in case of severe storm or heavy rain?
Our priority is respecting the American Flag and ensuring volunteer safety. In the rare event of torrential storms, placement may be shifted or retrieved early in accordance with the U.S. Flag Code.
Can I pay by check instead of online credit card?
Yes! When you click "Subscribe Now", select "Mail-In Check" to generate a printable subscription invoice slip and mail it to Friends of Fairview, 510 State HWY 5, Fairview, TX 75069.
What if I move during the year?
If you relocate within Fairview, we gladly transfer your remaining service to your new address at zero additional cost. Just email info@friendsoffairview.org.
